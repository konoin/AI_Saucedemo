## Name MP-03 - Access inventory page after successful login

## Priority
High

## Type
Positive

## Steps

1. 1. Log in with valid credentials (standard_user / secret_sauce).
2. 2. From the inventory page, verify URL is /inventory.html.
3. 3. Refresh the inventory page to ensure content reloads.
4. 4. Verify navigation to other inventory items or product detail pages works.

## Expected Result

Inventory page and its elements load correctly after login and navigation works. URLs are reachable and content is displayed without additional authentication prompts.
