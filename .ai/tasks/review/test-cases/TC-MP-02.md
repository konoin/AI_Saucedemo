High

## Type

1. Open application and login with standard_user / secret_sauce
2. Add three different products to the cart (e.g., Backpack, Bike Light, Bolt T-Shirt)
3. Click the cart icon to open the cart
4. Verify all three products appear in the cart with correct names and prices
5. Click 'Checkout'
6. Fill checkout information: First Name = 'Alice', Last Name = 'Smith', Postal Code = '90210'
7. Click 'Continue', verify overview lists all three products with aggregated total and tax calculation
