## Name MP-02 - Successful checkout with multiple products

## Priority
High

## Type
Positive

## Steps

1. Open SauceDemo and login with valid credentials
2. On the products page add multiple products (e.g., Sauce Labs Backpack and Sauce Labs Bike Light) to the cart
3. Click the cart icon to open the cart
4. Verify all selected products are listed with correct quantities and prices, then click Checkout
5. Enter valid checkout information (First Name, Last Name, Postal Code) and click Continue
6. On Overview verify individual item prices, subtotal and total (including tax if applicable) are correct, then click Finish
7. Verify the order confirmation page is displayed with success message

## Expected Result

User can purchase multiple items in a single checkout; overview shows correct items and totals; order confirmation is shown.
