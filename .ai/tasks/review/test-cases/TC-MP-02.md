# MP-02 - Successful checkout with multiple products

## Priority
High

## Type
Positive

## Steps

1. Precondition: Browser is open and clear of previous session data.
2. 1. Go to SauceDemo and log in with username = 'standard_user' and password = 'secret_sauce'.
3. 2. On the products page, add multiple products (e.g., 'Sauce Labs Backpack' and 'Sauce Labs Bolt T-Shirt') by clicking 'Add to cart' for each.
4. 3. Click the cart icon to open the cart page.
5. 4. Verify all added products appear in the cart with correct quantities.
6. 5. Click 'Checkout'.
7. 6. On 'Checkout: Your Information', enter First Name = 'Alice', Last Name = 'Smith', Postal Code = '12345' and click 'Continue'.
8. 7. On the 'Checkout: Overview' page, verify all items, their individual prices, subtotal, tax, and total are correct.
9. 8. Click 'Finish' to complete the purchase.

## Expected Result

Order completes and confirmation page is shown. The summary/order confirmation reflects purchase; cart badge shows zero items. All added items were included in the overview and removed from cart after completion.
