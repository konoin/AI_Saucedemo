## Name MP-07 - Cart persistence when navigating away and back

## Priority
Medium

## Type
Edge

## Steps

1. Log in to SauceDemo with valid credentials.
2. Add one or more products to the cart.
3. Navigate away from the cart (e.g., click 'Products' to return to product list) and then reopen the cart page.
4. Verify items and quantities match what was previously added.

## Expected Result

Cart contents persist across navigation within the session. Items added before navigating away remain in the cart with correct quantities and prices (no unexpected cart data loss).
