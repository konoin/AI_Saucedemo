## Name MP-01 - Successful checkout with single product

## Priority
High

## Type
Positive

## Steps

1. Open SauceDemo application in a supported browser
2. On the login page enter valid credentials (e.g., username: standard_user, password: secret_sauce) and click Login
3. On the products page locate a product (e.g., Sauce Labs Backpack) and click Add to cart
4. Click the cart icon to open the cart
5. Verify the selected product is listed in the cart and click Checkout
6. On the Checkout: Your Information page enter valid First Name (e.g., John), Last Name (e.g., Doe), and Postal Code (e.g., 90210) then click Continue
7. On the Checkout: Overview page verify product, price and total are displayed correctly and click Finish
8. Verify the order completion page is displayed and contains confirmation text (e.g., 'THANK YOU FOR YOUR ORDER')

## Expected Result

User is able to login, add a product to cart, complete checkout steps and reach the order confirmation page showing a successful purchase message. Cart should be cleared or show appropriate state after completion.
