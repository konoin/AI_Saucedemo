Name MP-01 - Successful checkout with a single product

## Priority

High

## Type

Positive

## Steps

1. Open SauceDemo application in a supported browser.
2. On the login page, enter username: 'standard_user' and password: 'secret_sauce' and click 'Login'.
3. Verify the products/products-list page is displayed.
4. Locate the product 'Sauce Labs Backpack' and click 'Add to cart'.
5. Verify the cart badge shows '1'.
6. Click the cart icon to open the cart page.
7. Verify 'Sauce Labs Backpack' is listed in the cart with correct name and price.
8. Click 'Checkout'.
9. On the 'Checkout: Your Information' page, enter First name: 'John', Last name: 'Doe', Postal Code: '12345' and click 'Continue'.
10. On the 'Checkout: Overview' page, verify the product, quantity, subtotal, tax and total are displayed and correct.
11. Click 'Finish'.

## Expected Result

Order completes successfully. User is taken to the complete/confirmation page and a confirmation message is displayed (e.g., 'THANK YOU FOR YOUR ORDER'). The completed order shows confirmation content and no errors occur during the flow.
