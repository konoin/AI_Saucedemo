## Name MP-01 - Successful checkout with single product

## Priority
High

## Type
Positive

## Steps

1. Open application at https://www.saucedemo.com
2. Login with valid credentials: username = 'standard_user', password = 'secret_sauce'
3. On the products page, click 'Add to cart' for a product (e.g., 'Sauce Labs Backpack')
4. Click the cart icon to open the cart
5. Verify the product is listed in the cart
6. Click 'Checkout'
7. On Checkout: Your Information page, enter First Name = 'John', Last Name = 'Doe', Postal Code = '12345'
8. Click 'Continue'
9. On the Checkout: Overview page, verify product, price and total are displayed
10. Click 'Finish'

## Expected Result

User is taken to the checkout complete page and sees a confirmation message ('THANK YOU FOR YOUR ORDER' or equivalent). Order is recorded (order complete flow finishes) and no validation errors occur.
