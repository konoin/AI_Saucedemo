# MP-01 - Successful checkout - single product

## Priority
High

## Type
Positive

## Steps

1. Open SauceDemo application.
2. Log in with valid credentials (standard_user / secret_sauce).
3. On the products page, click 'Add to cart' for a single product (e.g., 'Sauce Labs Backpack').
4. Verify cart badge increments to 1.
5. Click the shopping cart icon to open the cart.
6. Verify the selected product appears in the cart with correct name and price.
7. Click 'Checkout'.
8. On the Checkout: Your Information page, enter valid First Name, Last Name, and Postal Code.
9. Click 'Continue'.
10. On the Checkout Overview page, verify product details and total price (item total + tax) are correct.
11. Click 'Finish'.

## Expected Result

User is shown an order confirmation page with message 'THANK YOU FOR YOUR ORDER' (or equivalent). Order details reflect the purchased product and totals. No errors occur during the flow.
