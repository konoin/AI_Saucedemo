## Name MP-04 - Access inventory page after login (direct navigation)

## Priority
High

## Type
Positive

## Steps

1. 1. Navigate to https://www.saucedemo.com/ and login with valid credentials (standard_user / secret_sauce).
2. 2. After successful login, copy the Inventory page URL (/inventory.html) and navigate to it directly (or open a new tab and paste URL).
3. 3. Verify the page loads and displays inventory items.

## Expected Result

Direct navigation to /inventory.html while authenticated succeeds and inventory content is displayed. No additional login required while session is active.
