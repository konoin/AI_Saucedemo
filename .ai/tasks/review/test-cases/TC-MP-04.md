## Name MP-04 - Access inventory page after login by direct navigation

## Priority
Medium

## Type
Positive

## Steps

1. Precondition: User is logged in (standard_user).
2. Step 1: In the browser address bar navigate directly to /inventory.html.
3. Step 2: Verify that the inventory page loads correctly and shows expected items.

## Expected Result

Direct navigation to /inventory.html while logged in successfully displays the inventory page without additional authentication prompts.
